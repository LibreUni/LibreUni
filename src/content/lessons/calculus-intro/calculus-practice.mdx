## Problem Workshop

This workshop generates algorithmic practice problems spanning single-variable calculus: limits and continuity, differential calculus and the Mean Value Theorem, Riemann integration and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, and Taylor polynomials with remainder bounds.

### Recommended Workflow

1. Derive complete solutions on paper before inspecting hints.
2. Consult progressive hints when reaching an algebraic or conceptual impasse.
3. Verify derivations against the complete worked solution, checking hypothesis conditions and domain restrictions.
